Bidcraft

Bidcraft

A SaaS bid estimator for general contractors. Users build construction bids with line-item labor, material and equipment pricing plus markup, track each bid from draft to won or lost, and manage Free, Pro or Team plans through Stripe billing. Every table is locked down with Postgres row-level security.

  • Next.js
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Supabase
  • Postgres
  • Stripe
